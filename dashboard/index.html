One sealed snapshot of every protocol DefiLlama tracks. This board keeps only the protocols that record no token and are not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades, then ranks them by an implied launch value computed from the snapshot's own figures. Click any row for the full breakdown and the farm plan.

How the radar scores

Implied value is the entry's own claim, never a market price, and it never predicts whether or when a token is issued. It is computed from five factors, all derived from the snapshot at build time, with no protocol name ever special-cased:

implied = TVL x catMultiple x growthMult x maturityMult x chainMult x depthMult

  • catMultiple — the category's live-token mcap/TVL median, shrunk toward the global median by peer count: (n x median + 4 x global) / (n + 4), clamped to 0.05–1.0. A category with one live peer cannot pin the board to that peer's ratio; a category with no live peer falls back to the global median.
  • growthMult — 1 + growth x 0.009 when TVL rose over seven days, 1 + growth x 0.012 when it fell (growth clamped to -60..120%). Users leaving is a louder signal than users arriving, so a shrinking protocol loses value faster than a growing one gains it.
  • maturityMult — 0.55 + 0.35 x min(age, 1095)/1095 - 0.10 x years past three. One to three years with no token is prime: young enough to still launch, old enough to have proved it lasts. A protocol past four years has had its chances, so its factor decays; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • chainMult — 1 + 0.015 x chains (at most 8) + 0.02 when Ethereum is one of them. More chains mean more places to farm and deeper liquidity to launch into.
  • depthMult — 0.95 + 0.10 x n/(n+5), where n is the category's live-peer count. A category where tokens already trade has proven demand for the next one.

How the farm plans are built

Every row carries seven steps, each with a cost and a time. Capital follows the protocol's TVL: $300 above $5B, $200 above $1B, $100 above $100M, $50 below that. Gas is priced per chain (Ethereum about $12 a transaction, most L2s under $0.50, Solana about $0.02), the hold is 60 days for protocols under six months old, 45 days under a year, 30 days after that, and the exit is always two withdrawals a day apart. The action itself follows the category: a curator vault for risk curators, a supply position for lenders, a liquidity position for DEXs, and so on.

What is left out, and why

Bridges, canonical bridges, liquid staking and restaking receipts, restaked BTC, exchanges, versioned pools and names carrying wrapped, staked or staking are set aside: a receipt is not a launch waiting to happen. The board also separates the two sides of the market: the Pre-token tab ranks what may come, the Live token tab shows the 24 largest protocols whose token already trades, with the market's own capitalisation, so the multiples above can be checked against real prices.
